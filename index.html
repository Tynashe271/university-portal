Questions answered

Frequently asked questions.

Can’t find what you need? Our office will be happy to help.

Which forms can I apply for?+

The online enquiry form supports Forms 1–6. Places depend on availability and entry requirements.

Which documents will I need?+

Prepare a copy of the learner’s birth certificate, latest school report and guardian identification. The office may request additional documents.

Does submitting guarantee a place?+

No. Submission starts the review process. The admissions office confirms all successful placements directly.

Can I visit the school first?+

Yes. Contact the school office to arrange a suitable time or attend an advertised open morning.
